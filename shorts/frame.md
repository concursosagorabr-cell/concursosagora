# Concursos Agora — Shorts frame system

## Overview

A newsroom bulletin with the restraint of a public-service poster: deep navy ground,
warm paper-white type, gold as the signal colour and red reserved for the warning
beat. The tone is urgent but factual. The video should feel like a verified update
from a trusted desk, not a hype reel.

## The frame

- One persistent dark stage, with a faint INSS photograph and a measured grid in the
  background. The photograph is evidence, not wallpaper.
- Gold is the one accent that carries emphasis. Red appears only for the
  "ainda não há edital" warning and for the remanescentes slice of the data bar.
- Square corners, hard rules and offset shadows. No soft SaaS cards, no rounded
  glass containers, no gradient text.
- Display type is Archivo Black at poster scale. Metadata and labels are IBM Plex
  Mono, uppercase, wide-tracked, like a wire-service slug.
- The visual rhythm is: evidence → number → breakdown → warning → source → CTA.
  Each beat has one focal point; supporting detail is smaller and quieter.

## Composition rules

- Keep a 84px side safe margin and never place essential text inside the bottom
  220px, which YouTube Shorts' UI can cover.
- Numbers must be paired with a graphic (bar, split, bracket) so they do not float.
- The INSS photo is always credited on screen as `Foto: INSS`.
- The source line is `Fonte: INSS • 31 JUL 2026`; do not invent a schedule or
  posse date.
- The CTA must be readable for at least five seconds and include both the site and
  the social profile.
- No emojis, no fake notification badges, no "link na bio" that we cannot verify.

## Do not

- Do not call this a new concurso with open inscriptions. It is an authorisation
  for nomeações; the article says there is no edital de convocação yet.
- Do not say "vagas abertas", "inscrições abertas" or promise a date.
- Do not use pure black or pure white; use the defined ink and ground.
